# Lauren's Nutrition Plan

## Primary Goals
- 100 g protein per day minimum
- 25–35 g fiber per day minimum
- Less than 25 g added sugar per day
- Eating window: 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- Support weight loss, PCOS, LDL reduction, gut health, and GLP-1 success

## Daily Nutrition Targets

| Target | Amount |
|----------|----------|
| Protein | 100+ g |
| Fiber | 25–35 g |
| Added Sugar | <25 g |
| Water | 80–120 oz |
| Vegetables | 5+ servings |
| Fruit | 1–2 servings |
| Meals | 3 protein-focused meals |

## Meal Structure

### Meal 1 (Noon)
Target:
- 30–35 g protein
- 8–10 g fiber

Examples:
- Chicken breast + roasted vegetables
- Tuna pouch + salad
- Protein smoothie with fruit and spinach
- Eggs plus egg whites with vegetables

### Meal 2 (3–5 PM)
Target:
- 30–35 g protein
- 8–10 g fiber

Examples:
- Grilled chicken wrap
- Lean beef or turkey bowl
- Cottage cheese with fruit
- High-protein soup and vegetables

### Meal 3 (6–8 PM)
Target:
- 30–40 g protein
- 8–12 g fiber

Examples:
- Chicken, fish, or pork loin
- Sweet potato or beans
- Large serving of vegetables

## Foods to Emphasize

### Protein
- Chicken breast
- Chicken thighs (trimmed)
- Turkey breast
- Tuna
- Salmon
- Shrimp
- Lean pork loin
- Eggs and egg whites
- Cottage cheese
- Protein shakes

### Fiber
- Broccoli
- Green beans
- Brussels sprouts
- Carrots
- Mixed greens
- Apples
- Berries
- Pears
- Oats
- Beans
- Chickpeas

### Healthy Fats
- Avocado
- Olive oil
- Nuts
- Seeds

## Foods to Reduce

- Sugary drinks
- Excess sweets
- Highly processed snack foods
- Frequent fast food
- Large amounts of red meat
- Excess dairy
- Excess fried foods

## Non-Negotiables

1. Eat protein first at every meal.
2. Reach 100 g protein before worrying about calories.
3. Reach at least 25 g fiber daily.
4. Drink water consistently throughout the day.
5. Sleep 8 hours per night.
6. Take medications and supplements consistently.
7. Prioritize whole foods over packaged foods.
