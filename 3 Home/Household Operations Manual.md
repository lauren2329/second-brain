## Zone System (FlyLady-inspired)

Work 15–30 min per zone. Timer-based. Progress over perfection.

**Rules:**

- Set timer for 15 minutes
- Work only in assigned zone
- Stop when timer ends
- Do not pull out more than can be put away

### Zone 1 – Entry, Outdoor & Auto Care

Areas: Front porch, front door, back patio, fireplace, seasonal décor, outdoor storage, vehicle maintenance

### Zone 2 – Kitchen, Pantry & Laundry

Areas: Sink, counters, stove, refrigerator, pantry, laundry supplies, washer/dryer

### Zone 3 – Bathrooms, Hallway & Stairs

Areas: Upstairs bathroom, downstairs bathroom, hallway, stairs, linen storage

### Zone 4 – Family Spaces

Areas: Living room, dining room, playroom

### Zone 5 – Office, Bedrooms & Personal Spaces

Areas: Office, master bedroom, Lily's room, boys' room, closets, dressers

**Each zone has:**

- Weekly checklist: declutter surfaces, remove trash, dust, vacuum/sweep, wipe doors/switches, organize one drawer/cabinet, put away misplaced items, identify repairs
- Deep cleaning: baseboards, windows, window tracks, walls, behind/under furniture, light fixtures, storage areas, seasonal rotation, donation box review
