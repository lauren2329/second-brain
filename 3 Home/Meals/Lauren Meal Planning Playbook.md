# Nutrition Goals

## Current Priorities

- Support weight loss while using a GLP-1 medication strategy.
- Improve LDL cholesterol.
- Support PCOS.
- Increase protein intake.
- Increase fiber intake.
- Improve gut microbiome diversity.
- Reduce highly processed foods.
- Reduce added sugar.

## Daily Targets

### Protein
- Goal: 100g protein per day minimum.
- Preferred structure:
  - Breakfast/Lunch Window: 30g+
  - Dinner: 30g+
  - Additional snacks to reach target.

### Fiber
- Goal: 25–35g daily.
- Minimum acceptable: 25g.

### Added Sugar
- Target: Under 25g added sugar daily.

### Hydration
- Prioritize water first.
- Increase fluid intake during weight-loss efforts.

# Ramen Upgrade Method

Discussed for feeding the kids quickly.

## Protein First

Use chicken.

## Fast Teriyaki Hack

Instead of marinating:

Combine:
- Soy sauce
- Brown sugar or honey
- Garlic
- Ginger

Cook chicken first.

Add sauce at the end.

Reduce until glossy.

Slice and serve over ramen.

### Shortcut Version

1. Dice chicken.
2. Season lightly.
3. Cook in skillet.
4. Add teriyaki ingredients.
5. Simmer 2–3 minutes.
6. Serve over prepared ramen.
