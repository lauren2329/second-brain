# Monthly Strategic Review

## Purpose

The Monthly Strategic Review keeps the full ecosystem aligned.

It is not a task list. It is a strategy check.

## Core Question

Are my organizations, projects, finances, household, and health moving in the right direction?

## Review Areas

### Revenue

Review:

- Income produced
- Pending invoices
- Sponsorship pipeline
- Consulting opportunities
- Event revenue
- Grant opportunities
- Donor prospects

### Growth

Review:

- Audience growth
- Email list growth
- Partner growth
- Sponsor growth
- Community growth
- Brand visibility

### Projects

For each major project:

- Status
- Progress
- Blockers
- Timeline
- Next decision
- Continue / pause / cut

### Relationships

Review:

- Key partners
- Donors
- sponsors
- collaborators
- legislators
- volunteers
- vendors

### Health

Review:

- Energy
- capacity
- medication changes
- sleep
- pain
- stress
- executive function load

### Family / Household

Review:

- Homeschool
- appointments
- kids' needs
- house systems
- transportation
- pets

### Risk

Review:

- Compliance
- legal
- tax
- finances
- missed deadlines
- unstable systems
- overloaded commitments

## Executive Summary
