# Daily Executive Brief

## Purpose

This system exists to convert scattered information into a clear daily command brief.

The goal is not to summarize everything. The goal is to identify what requires attention, action, decision, follow-up, or protection of time.

## Inputs

Use available sources when accessible:

- Calendar
- Gmail
- Slack or messages
- GitHub
- Obsidian / knowledge base
- Tasks
- Recent notes
- Project documents

## Output Standard

Only include information that is:

- Actionable
- Time-sensitive
- Strategically important
- Related to commitments
- Related to money, people, deadlines, or risk

Do not include low-value information simply because it exists.

## Daily Brief Prompt

Generate a Daily Executive Brief.

Act as my Chief of Staff, Operations Director, and Knowledge Manager.

Your job is not to summarize information. Your job is to identify what requires my attention today.

Review all available sources including:

- Calendar
- Email
- GitHub
- Obsidian / Knowledge Base
- Tasks
- Messages
- Project notes

Only include information that is actionable, time-sensitive, important, or strategically relevant.

### Executive Summary

Provide a 3-5 sentence overview of:

- What matters most today
- Any major risks
- Any approaching deadlines
- Any important opportunities

### Today's Schedule

List all appointments, meetings, events, and deadlines.

For each item include:

- Time
- People involved
- Purpose
- Preparation required

Hide informational calendar events that require no action.

### Critical Communications

Only include:

- Emails requiring a response
- Messages requiring a response
- Mentions or requests directed at me
- Sponsor, partner, donor, client, or stakeholder communications

Group by:

- Urgent
- Important
- Can Wait

### Open Loops

Identify:

- Unfinished conversations
- Outstanding commitments
- Waiting-on items
- Delegated items needing follow-up

Include:

- Who owns the next step
- Recommended action

### Active Projects

For each active project provide:

- Current status
- Next action
- Blockers
- Deadline if applicable

Prioritize:

- Kentucky NORML
- Common Good Network
- Portal to Wellness
- Wonderland Wellness World
- VMP
- Kentucky Cannabis Conference
- Bluegrass Elevated
- Household Operations

### Risks & Deadlines

List:

- Missed commitments
- Upcoming deadlines
- Funding concerns
- Compliance concerns
- Events approaching without preparation

### Top 3 Priorities

Identify the three highest-leverage actions for today.

Each priority must include:

- Why it matters
- Expected outcome
- Estimated time required

### Decision Queue

List any decisions that appear to be waiting on me.

For each decision include:

- Context
- Options
- Recommendation

### Knowledge Base Updates

Identify:

- New notes that should become permanent records
- New people to add to CRM
- New projects
- New SOPs
- New recurring tasks
- Contradictions or unresolved questions discovered in notes

## Operating Rule

If a briefing item does not help me decide, act, delegate, or protect time, omit it.
