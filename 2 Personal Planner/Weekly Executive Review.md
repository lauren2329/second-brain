# Weekly Executive Review

## Purpose

The Weekly Executive Review prevents drift.

It answers:

- What moved?
- What stalled?
- What needs a decision?
- What needs to be cut, delegated, scheduled, or escalated?

## Review Cadence

Recommended: Sunday reset or Monday morning.

## Inputs

- Daily briefs from the past week
- Calendar
- Email
- Project notes
- GitHub / Obsidian changes
- Revenue notes
- Household commitments

## Review Questions

### 1. What moved forward?

List wins, progress, completed tasks, and meaningful conversations.

### 2. What stalled?

Identify stuck projects, unanswered messages, unfinished commitments, and blocked tasks.

### 3. What generated money or opportunity?

Track:

- Sales conversations
- Sponsorship leads
- Donor leads
- Consulting opportunities
- Event revenue
- New partnerships

### 4. What consumed time without producing value?

Name the drain directly.

Examples:

- Admin loops
- Unclear meetings
- Unstructured messages
- Duplicated tools
- Emotional labor
- Avoidable errands

### 5. What needs a decision?

Create a decision queue.

For each decision:

- Context
- Options
- Recommendation
- Deadline

### 6. What needs delegation?

List anything that should not remain on Lauren's plate.

### 7. What should stop?

Identify tasks, projects, commitments, or tools that are no longer worth the cost.

### 8. What should start?

List only high-value starts.

### 9. What must happen next week?

Limit to the essential priorities.

## Weekly Output Template

```markdown
# Weekly Executive Review - YYYY-MM-DD

## Executive Summary

## Progress

## Stalled Items

## Revenue & Opportunity

## Time Drains

## Decision Queue

## Delegation List

## Stop / Start / Continue

## Top Priorities for Next Week

## Knowledge Base Updates
```
