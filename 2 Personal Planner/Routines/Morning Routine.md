## Morning Routine

1. **Wake Up and Refresh 🌞**
    - **Wake Up Naturally or with a Gentle Alarm ⏰:** Allow yourself to wake up gently.
    - **Bed Airing 🛏️:** Pull back the covers and let the bed air out for about 30 minutes.
    - **Hydrate 💧:** Drink a glass of water to kickstart your metabolism.

2. **Morning Hygiene Routine 🚿**
    - **Shower & Clean the Bathroom 🛁:** Take a refreshing shower and give the bathroom a quick wipe-down.
    - **Swish the Toilet 🚽:** A quick swish keeps it clean with minimal effort.
    - **Skincare & Get Dressed 👗:** Follow your skincare routine and dress for the day.

3. **Breakfast and Day Planning 🍽️**
    - **Prepare and Eat Breakfast 🍳:** Make a nutritious breakfast, and sit down to enjoy it mindfully.
    - **Plan the Day 📅:** While eating, review your Google Calendar and Todoist for the day's tasks. Begin updating your daily log in Obsidian.

4. **Morning Family Time 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦**
    - **Children's Homeschool Prep 🎒:** Set up any necessary materials for the kids' homeschool activities.
    - **Family Interaction 🧸:** Spend a few minutes engaging with your children and husband.

5. **Work/Business Start-Up 💼**
    - **Obsidian Review 🗂️:** Check your project notes and tasks in Obsidian to align with your work priorities.
    - **Priority Work Tasks 🚀:** Dive into the most critical tasks first, ensuring a productive start.

6. **Household Maintenance 🌻**
    - **FlyLady Morning Routine 🧹:** Work on FlyLady's Zone tasks for 15 minutes or follow the daily mission.
    - **Start Laundry 👕:** Put a load of laundry in to keep on top of household chores.

7. **Personal Time & Self-Care 💆‍♀️**
    - **Self-Care Session 🧘‍♀️:** Dedicate a few minutes to personal wellness, whether it's stretching, meditation, or a quick skincare routine.
    - **Obsidian Personal Goals 📓:** Check your daily log for self-care and personal goals to ensure you stay aligned with your needs.
