# Portal to Wellness

## Overview

Portal to Wellness is an educational platform connected to Common Good Network.

It is intended to support accessible wellness education, community resources, and practical guidance.

## Purpose

Provide consumer-friendly education and support around wellness topics, including cannabis and hemp education where appropriate.
