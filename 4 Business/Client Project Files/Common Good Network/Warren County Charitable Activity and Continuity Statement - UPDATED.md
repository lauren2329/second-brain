# Common Good Network Inc.
## Warren County Charitable Activity & Continuity Statement

Common Good Network Inc., formerly known as Kentucky Cannabis Foundation, has operated charitable and educational programs in Kentucky for more than three years. During that period, the organization's home office and operational coordination have been based in Warren County, Kentucky.

Since 2024, Common Good Network has operated Portal to Wellness (portaltowellness.org), a free, public educational resource providing health and wellness information to Kentucky residents and the general public. Portal to Wellness operates independently of any other partner organization and represents Common Good Network's own ongoing educational programming.

For the last three years, Common Good Network has partnered with Kentucky NORML to help fund and co-host the Kentucky Cannabis Conference. The conference is an educational event that brings together thought leaders from around the country to educate the public, regulators, legislators, patients, and industry participants on issues involving cannabis policy and the emerging cannabis industry, including regulatory matters, compliance, legal issues, advocacy, best practices, and patient education. During this three-year period, the conference was held twice in Frankfort, Kentucky, at the Capitol Plaza Hotel, and once in Louisville, Kentucky, at the Kentucky International Convention Center. Across those three years, the conference served approximately 700 attendees. Common Good Network contributed $10,000 toward the conference's costs in 2023, $13,000 in 2024, and $15,000 in 2025.

In 2025, Common Good Network also conducted a series of free patient-registration drives to assist patients with access to Kentucky's medical cannabis program. These events were held from August through November 2025 in London, Morehead, Bowling Green, and Paducah, Kentucky, at a total cost of approximately $8,000. Event locations included the London Community Center, The Center for Health, Education & Research, the Sloan Convention Center, and the Paducah-McCracken County Convention & Expo Center. Through these patient drives, the organization helped more than 350 patients register for the program at no cost.

Common Good Network's home office has been exclusively in Warren County. The organization's operations were coordinated from Bowling Green locations, including the Lisa Rice Library and the organization's home office. From September 2025 through April 2026, the organization's mail was received through a post office box in Cave City, Barren County. That mailing arrangement was handled by former director Linda Buchanan, who lived in that area and assisted with mail handling. The Cave City address was used for mail handling only and did not change the organization's Warren County operational base.

Common Good Network has recently updated its bylaws, internal policies, governance practices, and recordkeeping procedures as part of its nonprofit compliance work. Going forward, the organization is strengthening board oversight, financial controls, document retention, and compliance procedures to support its charitable mission and any charitable gaming activities for which it may apply.

Supporting records for the organization's conferences, patient-registration drives, Portal to Wellness, and Warren County operations are available upon request.
