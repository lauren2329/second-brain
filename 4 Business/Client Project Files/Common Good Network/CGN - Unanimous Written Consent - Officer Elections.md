The undersigned, being all of the directors of Common Good Network Inc. Ashley Priest is removed from / resigns from the Board of Directors, effective as of the date of this consent.

### FURTHER RESOLVED, Disclosure of Related Parties

The Board acknowledges, for the record and for purposes of the Corporation's Conflict of Interest Policy (Bylaws Article VII), the following relationships among current directors and officers:
- Annabel Lauren Kie and Matthew Bratcher are spouses.
- Joan Bratcher is Matthew Bratcher's mother.
- Mandy Liles and Harry Ezajian are spouses.

The Board further acknowledges that these relationships shall be taken into account in determining director disinterestedness for any future transaction requiring disinterested-director approval under Bylaws Sections 7.2–7.3 and 8.4, and that such approvals shall be documented separately from this consent as each transaction arises.

Harry Ezajian, as Treasurer, shall have no role in approving, processing, or facilitating any reimbursement to himself for funds he personally advanced to the Corporation. Any such reimbursement requires separate written approval by a disinterested director (Joan Bratcher, as the only director unrelated to Harry Ezajian and Mandy Liles) before any payment is made.

### FURTHER RESOLVED, Filings

The officers are authorized and directed to take all actions necessary to reflect the foregoing changes in the Corporation's filings with the Kentucky Secretary of State and the Kentucky Office of Charitable Gaming.
