**CORRECTED (was overstated as "confirmed required" — actual regulation is more nuanced):** Kentucky Charitable Gaming does have a separate **Form CG-APP-FAC — Charitable Gaming Facility License Application** (820 KAR 1:005), but it's not automatically required just because you have a gaming location. Per 820 KAR 1:001, a "primary office location" — the building where the organization conducts the majority of its charitable business — is treated differently from a licensed "charitable gaming facility," which the regulations describe in terms of a lease between the facility and the charitable organization (i.e., typically a third-party-operated venue, often used by multiple organizations, like a commercial bingo hall). If Golden Door is Common Good Network's own location that it operates directly — not leased from an independent facility operator — **you likely do not need a separate CG-APP-FAC**; it would simply be your listed gaming location on this ORG application (Q21).

**The one thing that changes this:** the "primary office location" carve-out explicitly excludes any space shared with a bar, restaurant, convenience store, or other commercial retail business. If Golden Door shares its physical space with any such business, it falls outside that carve-out and would need its own facility license regardless of who operates it.

**Answered, and it's a genuine gray area — recommend explicit OCG confirmation before filing.** There's another office in the same renovated garage building: an import car lot office, with its own entrance, bathroom, and parking lot, and zero cross-access between the two spaces.

- **In favor of "no facility license needed":** the total physical separation matches what the "primary office location" exclusion seems concerned with avoiding — commingled, shared gaming space (like gaming happening in the same room as a bar). Two independently accessed units in one building isn't that.
- **Against it:** a car lot sells vehicles directly to the public, which arguably makes it a "commercial retail business" in the plain sense — the same category the exclusion names (bar, restaurant, convenience store, "or other commercial retail business").

**Decision: proceeding without a separate CG-APP-FAC**, per Chuck's guidance based on his experience with charitable gaming orgs statewide. Golden Door will be listed as the organization's own gaming location on this CG-APP-ORG application (Q21) only.
