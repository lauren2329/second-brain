# Financing Planning Notes

> Planning document only. Not legal or tax advice. No contract language.

## Overview

The financing plan is divided into **two completely separate
arrangements**:

1.  **Business Bridge Loan** for startup funding.
2.  **Personal Debt Repayment** for an existing obligation.

Keeping these separate simplifies accounting, taxes, and documentation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Business Bridge Loan

## Lender

Jeremy

## Loan Amount

**\$40,000**

## Purpose

Provide startup capital immediately after the charitable gaming license
is approved so VMP can prepare the gaming lounge for opening while
waiting for reimbursement from the Arrow-funded project.

## Expected Timeline

-   License approved (approximately two weeks)
-   Jeremy funds **\$40,000** immediately
-   Approximately 30 days to complete build-out
-   Doors open
-   Arrow reimbursement expected up to approximately 45 days after
    opening
-   VMP repays Jeremy in full plus the agreed short-term return

This functions as a short-term bridge loan.

## Planned Use of Funds

  Category                                  Amount
  --------------------------------- --------------
  Gaming cash till                        \$10,000
  Build-out, furnishings, signage         \$10,000
  Operating reserve                        \$8,000
  Owner draw (Lauren)                     \$12,000
  **Total**                           **\$40,000**

### Owner Draw

Current objective:

-   Stabilize personal finances quickly.
-   Catch up on essential household expenses.
-   Maintain enough business cash to complete the startup and operate
    until reimbursement arrives.

Assumption: - VMP remains an LLC (unless tax status changes later). - An
**owner draw/distribution** is the simplest planning assumption. - Final
tax treatment should be confirmed with the business CPA.

# Recommended Cash Flow Sequence

1.  Charitable gaming license approved.
2.  Jeremy funds the \$40,000 bridge loan.
3.  VMP completes build-out.
4.  Business opens.
5.  Arrow reimbursement is received.
6.  VMP repays Jeremy's bridge loan plus the agreed return.
7.  Continue the separate 18-month repayment plan for the personal
    \$25,000 debt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Planning Principles

-   Keep business and personal obligations separate.
-   Budget the owner draw intentionally instead of taking leftover cash.
-   Maintain sufficient operating capital until reimbursement arrives.
-   Repay the bridge loan promptly once funds are received.
-   Use a detailed cash-flow forecast before finalizing amounts.
