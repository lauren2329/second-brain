## Balanced Terms v2 (added 2026-07-16, to address fairness gap for Harry)

Unchanged from the original proposal: Harry buys ASAP; base rent = 2x mortgage; Phase 1 (pre-$50k repayment) rent is 100% his with no equity conceded; 10% permanent management/holding fee on rent.

New additions to fix the long-term imbalance (Harry currently hands off 100% of the property's upside once rent-to-own starts, which cuts against his stated goal of building wealth through real estate):

1. **Appreciation share at exit:** when title transfers to LMK (or the property is sold/refinanced), Harry receives an additional 20% of the appreciation since purchase (appraised value at transfer minus his purchase price). Gives him real upside participation, not just a rental fee.
2. **Carrying costs split:** Harry (titled owner) covers property tax and insurance; LMK (occupant/eventual owner) covers routine maintenance and repairs — standard rent-to-own convention, protects his net margin.
3. **Downside protection for Harry:** if the arrangement falls apart before the $50k is repaid or before LMK is fully vested, Harry keeps the property and all rent paid to date — no clawback.
