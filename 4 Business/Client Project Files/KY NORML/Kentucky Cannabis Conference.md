# Kentucky Cannabis Conference

## Overview

Kentucky Cannabis Conference is Kentucky NORML's major annual cannabis reform and education event.

It was established in 2024 and is intended to be held during the first week of March in Louisville.

## Parent Organization

- [[Kentucky NORML]]

## Current Strategic Direction

The model has shifted away from an expo model.

Current concept:

- Conference-focused
- Meals-based networking
- Consumer choice awards
- Directory listings
- Sponsor-driven
- Fewer unnecessary breakouts
- Higher-value relationship building

## Core Components

- Main conference sessions
- Sponsor visibility
- Networking meals
- Consumer choice awards
- Directory listing opportunities
