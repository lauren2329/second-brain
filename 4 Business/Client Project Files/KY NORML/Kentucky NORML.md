## Overview

Kentucky NORML is a 501(c)(4) cannabis policy reform organization focused on advocacy, education, and reform in Kentucky.

Lauren serves as Deputy Director.

## Purpose

Advance cannabis policy reform, public education, patient access, and community engagement in Kentucky.
