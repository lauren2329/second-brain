
Creating an organized file structure in Obsidian based on different areas of your life will help you efficiently manage your time and tasks. Below is a suggested file structure, categorized by key areas such as Personal, Work, Homeschool, Family, and more. Each area will have its own set of folders and subfolders, with templates or notes organized within them.

### **1. Personal 🧘‍♀️** 
 - **1.1 Self-Care 💆‍♀️**
	 - 1.1.1 **Morning Routine 🌅**
	    - FlyLady Morning Routine 🛏️: Detailed checklist following the FlyLady r outine.
	- 1.1.2 **Evening Routine 🌙**
	    - FlyLady Evening Routine 🧽: Checklist for evening tasks like tidying up and planning for tomorrow.
- 1.2 **Goals 🎯**
	- Goals 
- 1.3 **Journaling 🖋️**
	- Review
	- Life Timeline
	- Trackers
	- Daily Journal Entry
- 1.4 **Hobbies & Interests 🎨
	- **Creative Projects 🎨**
	    - Plan and track progress on personal creative projects.
- 1.5 **Personal Development 🚀**
	- **Learning & Courses 📖**
		- Track ongoing learning, courses, and educational goals.
	- **Skills & Certifications 🏅**
	    - Track skills development and certifications.
	- **Reading List 📚**
		- Track books to read and reading progress.
### **2. Work 💼**

- **2.1 Hilltopper Hemp 🌱**    
    - **2.1.1 Marketing 📣**
        - **2.1.1.1 Social Media Calendar 📅:** Plan and track social media posts using a monthly spread.
        - **2.1.1.2 Content Creation 🎥:** Outline content ideas, video scripts, and image concepts.
        - **2.1.1.3 Ad Campaigns 💻:** Plan, budget, and analyze the performance of paid advertising campaigns.
        - **2.1.1.4 Email Marketing 📧:** Draft and schedule email campaigns, newsletters, and automated sequences.
    - **2.1.2 Operations 🛠️**
        - **2.1.2.1 Financials 💵:** Track weekly and monthly finances, including revenue, expenses, and cash flow.
        - **2.1.2.2 Inventory Management 📦:** Monitor stock levels, restocking schedules, and supplier contacts.
        - **2.1.2.3 Customer Relations 💬:** Track customer feedback, manage inquiries, and maintain service records.
        - **2.1.2.4 Vendor & Supplier Coordination 📜:** Keep records of supplier agreements, orders, and communications.
- **2.2 Kentucky NORML 🌿**    
    - **2.2.1 Fundraising 💰**
        - **2.2.1.1 Campaign Planner 🗂️:** Plan and track fundraising campaigns, including timelines and goals.
        - **2.2.1.2 Donor Management 🤝:** Record donor information, contributions, and follow-up actions.
        - **2.2.1.3 Grant Writing ✍️:** Draft, edit, and submit grant applications, including deadlines and required documents.
    - **2.2.2 Events 🎉**
        - **2.2.2.1 Event Planning 📅:** Template for planning events, including timelines, budgets, and logistics.
        - **2.2.2.2 Sponsorships & Partnerships 🤝:** Track and manage event sponsors, partners, and collaborators.
        - **2.2.2.3 Post-Event Analysis 📊:** Review event performance, including attendance, feedback, and financial outcomes.
- **2.3 Other Work-Related 📝**    
    - **2.3.1 Professional Development 📚**
        - **2.3.1.1 Course Tracker 🎓:** Track progress in courses and professional development programs.
        - **2.3.1.2 Certifications & Licenses 📜:** Maintain records of certifications, renewals, and licensing requirements.
        - **2.3.1.3 Networking & Conferences 🌐:** Record details of networking events, conferences, and professional meetups.
    - **2.3.2 Miscellaneous Work Tasks 🗒️**
        - **2.3.2.1 To-Do Lists 📝:** Temporary work-related tasks that need attention but don’t fit into other categories.
        - **2.3.2.2 Brainstorming & Ideas 💡:** Capture ideas for future projects, improvements, or innovations.

### **3. Homeschool 🎒**

- **3.1 Lesson Plans 📚**
    
    - **3.1.1 Daily Lesson Plan 📋:** Plan and track homeschooling lessons daily, including objectives and materials.
    - **3.1.2 Weekly Lesson Overview 📆:** Outline the week’s lesson objectives, materials, and goals for each subject.
    - **3.1.3 Field Trips & Activities 🎒:** Plan and record educational outings, field trips, and hands-on activities.
    - **3.1.4 Special Projects 🛠️:** Plan and track special homeschooling projects, experiments, or presentations.
- **3.2 Subjects 📖**
    
    - **3.2.1 Subject Trackers 📝:** Track progress in each subject, including completed lessons and upcoming topics.
    - **3.2.2 Subject Resources 📚:** Organize resources, books, and materials specific to each subject.
    - **3.2.3 Subject Goals 🎯:** Set and track academic goals for each subject, including milestones and achievements.
- **3.3 Resources 🧰**
    
    - **3.3.1 Weekly Resource Planner 📅:** Plan and gather resources needed for the week’s lessons and activities.
    - **3.3.2 Resource Library 📖:** Maintain a library of educational resources, including books, online tools, and supplies.
    - **3.3.3 Curriculum Research 🔍:** Record notes and findings from researching new curricula, programs, or methods.
- **3.4 Progress Tracking 📊**
    
    - **3.4.1 Assessment Log 🗒️:** Track academic progress with checklists, notes, and assessments.
    - **3.4.2 Report Cards 📄:** Create and store report cards or progress reports for each term or grading period.
    - **3.4.3 Achievement Records 🏆:** Record academic achievements, awards, and recognitions.
    - **3.4.4 Learning Challenges 🧩:** Track any learning difficulties, interventions, or support strategies.

### **4. Family 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦**

- **4.1 Family Calendar 🗓️**
    - **Monthly Calendar 📅:** Track family events.
- **4.2 Family Activities 🎨**
    - **Weekend Plans 🏞️:** Plan family activities.
- **4.3 Household Management 🏡**
    - **FlyLady Zone Tasks 🧽:** Track FlyLady’s zone cleaning tasks with weekly checklists.
    - **Monthly & Yearly Home Maintenance 🛠️:** Schedule and track home maintenance tasks.
- 4.4 **Meal Planning & Preferences 🍽️**
    - **Weekly Menu 🗒️:** Plan meals for the week.
    - **Preferred Foods 🍲:** List each family member's favorite foods and dietary restrictions.
- 4.5 **Medical Information 💊**
	- - **Health Records 🩺:** Track medical information, appointments, and health stats for each family member.
	- **Preferred Products 🛒:** List preferred healthcare and personal care products for each family member.
- 4.6 **Clothing & Shoe Sizes 👗👟**
	- **Sizes List 📏:** Record clothing and shoe sizes for all family members

4.7 **Auto Care 🚗**

- **Vehicle Maintenance Log 🧰:** Track regular maintenance, oil changes, tire rotations, and other auto care tasks.

4.8 **Pet Care 🐾**

- **Pet Information 🐶:** Record vet appointments, vaccinations, preferred food, and care routines for your pets.

### **5. Finances 💵**

- **5.1 Budgeting 💰**
    - **Monthly Budget 📊:** Track monthly expenses and income.
- **5.2 Expense Tracking 🧾**
    - **Daily Expense Log 📒:** Log daily expenses.
- **5.3 Investments & Savings 💸**
    - **Savings Goals 🎯:** Track savings and investments.

### **6. Health & Wellness 🏥**

- **6.1.1 Conditions & Treatments 🩺:** Document chronic conditions, ongoing treatments, and doctor recommendations.
    - **6.1.2 Medications 💊:** List prescribed and over-the-counter medications, including dosages, schedules, and side effects.
    - **6.1.3 Medical History 🗂️:** Record past surgeries, illnesses, allergies, and family medical history.
    - **6.1.4 Appointments & Checkups 📅:** Track upcoming and past medical appointments, including routine checkups and specialist visits.
    - **6.1.5 Health Insurance 🛡️:** Organize insurance details, including policy numbers, provider contacts, and claims history.
- **6.2 Fitness Tracker 🏋️‍♀️**
    
    - **6.2.1 Exercise Logs 📝:** Log daily workouts, including types of exercise, duration, and intensity.
    - **6.2.2 Workout Plans 📋:** Create and track personalized workout routines and fitness goals.
    - **6.2.3 Progress Tracking 📈:** Monitor fitness progress with measurements, weight tracking, and milestones.
    - **6.2.4 Sports & Activities 🏀:** Record participation in sports, recreational activities, and any related achievements.
- **6.3 Nutrition 🍎**
    
    - **6.3.1 Meal Plans 🗒️:** Plan and log daily meals, including breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks.
    - **6.3.2 Dietary Restrictions 🚫:** Document any allergies, intolerances, or special dietary needs.
    - **6.3.3 Nutritional Goals 🎯:** Set and track goals for macros, calorie intake, hydration, and specific nutrients.
    - **6.3.4 Recipes & Food Journal 📖:** Collect favorite recipes and maintain a food journal to track what you eat daily.
- **6.4 Mental Health 🧠**
    
    - **6.4.1 Mindfulness & Meditation 🧘‍♀️:** Track meditation practices, mindfulness exercises, and relaxation techniques.
    - **6.4.2 Stress Management 🌿:** Develop and monitor stress-reduction strategies, including breathing exercises, therapy, and journaling.
    - **6.4.3 Emotional Wellbeing 🌻:** Log moods, emotions, and any triggers that affect your mental health.
    - **6.4.4 Therapy & Counseling 🛋️:** Track therapy sessions, progress, and key takeaways from counseling.
- **6.5 Sleep & Rest 💤**
    
    - **6.5.1 Sleep Log 🛏️:** Record sleep patterns, duration, and quality, including any issues like insomnia or sleep apnea.
    - **6.5.2 Bedtime Routine 🌙:** Develop and track a bedtime routine to improve sleep hygiene.
    - **6.5.3 Rest & Recovery 🛀:** Plan for rest days, relaxation activities, and recovery techniques after physical exertion.

### **7. Projects 📂**

- **7.1 Current Projects 🛠️**
    
    - **7.1.1 Personal Projects 👤:** Ongoing personal projects such as hobbies, self-improvement tasks, or personal goals.
    - **7.1.2 Family Projects 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦:** Collaborative family projects like home renovations, family vacations, or group activities.
    - **7.1.3 Work Projects 💼:** Active work-related projects, including tasks for Hilltopper Hemp, Kentucky NORML, or other professional endeavors.
    - **7.1.4 Community Projects 🌍:** Projects involving community service, volunteer work, or local initiatives.
    - **7.1.5 Educational Projects 🎓:** Academic or learning projects, including courses, research, or homeschooling plans.
- **7.2 Completed Projects ✅**
    
    - **7.2.1 Archive 📦:** Archive of completed projects with summaries, lessons learned, and reflections.
    - **7.2.2 Milestones 🏅:** Record significant achievements or milestones reached in completed projects.
- **7.3 Future Ideas 💡**
    
    - **7.3.1 Brainstorming 🧠:** Space for brainstorming new project ideas, potential collaborations, or creative ventures.
    - **7.3.2 Wishlist 🎯:** List of desired future projects or goals, including long-term ambitions or bucket list items.
    - **7.3.3 Planning & Research 📋:** Initial planning stages and research notes for potential projects.

### **8. Miscellaneous 🗃️**
- **8.1 Random Notes 🗒️**
    
    - **8.1.1 Quick Thoughts 💭:** Jot down spontaneous ideas, reminders, or notes.
    - **8.1.2 To-Do Lists ✅:** Temporary or ad-hoc task lists that don’t fit into other categories.
- **8.2 Ideas & Inspiration 💡**
	- **8.2.1 Creative Sparks ✨:** Capture creative ideas, artistic inspirations, or innovative thoughts.
    - **8.2.2 Motivational Quotes 🗣️:** Collection of quotes, affirmations, or motivational sayings.
    - **8.2.3 Vision Board 🖼️:** Visual or written representation of your goals, aspirations, or dream projects.
- **8.3 Reference Materials 📚**
    - **8.3.1 Manuals & Guides 📖:** Store user manuals, instructional guides, and how-to documents.
    - **8.3.2 Articles & Research 📰:** Archive of important articles, research papers, and scholarly resources.
    - **8.3.3 Templates & Forms 🗂️:** Keep templates, forms, and reusable documents for easy access.
    - **8.3.4 Contacts & Resources 📇:** List of important contacts, suppliers, or resource links.
---

### **Central Dashboard in Obsidian 🗺️**

- **FlyLady Overview 🧽:** Links to all your FlyLady routines, categorized by daily, weekly, and zone tasks.
- **Bullet Journal Overview 📓:** Links to daily logs, habit trackers, and other bullet journal templates.
- **Quick Links to Key Areas 🔗:** Ensure your central dashboard has links to all your primary folders.

This structure, with the addition of emojis, will make it easier to quickly identify different areas and keep your Obsidian workspace organized and visually intuitive.
