## Summer Reading Farmers Market 2026

## Market Details

This weekly Saturday morning market is designed to showcase local farmers/growers, crafters/artisans, and food trucks. All products sold will be made/created/grown/cooked by individuals and small businesses.

**Additional Details:**

- **Location:** [**@ SOKY Center (link to page)**](https://www.sokybg.org/summer-reading-market) at 636 Center Street
- **Dates:** Every Saturday in June and July (minus July 4) and August 1
- **Hours:** 8 a.m. – 12 p.m.
- **Free parking**: Stadium Park Plaza's parking garage

Information about this market and others through the year can also be found at SOKY Center's **[web site.](https://www.sokybg.org/summer-reading-market)**

### Saturdays, weekly, from June 6 through August 1!

The Summer Reading Market is more than a farmers market—it's a celebration of community, healthy living, and the joy of reading. Hosted under the SOKY Pavilion during the warmer months, the market brings together local growers, makers, and library programming in one lively, family-friendly space.

From **June 6 through August 1** (excluding July 4), folks can shop on Saturday mornings (8 a.m. to 12 noon) for fresh produce, handmade creations, and local goods, as well as bring the whole family to enjoy pop-up storytimes, inflatables, costumed characters, and activities designed to keep all ages reading and thriving all summer long.

- Saturday, July 18, will be a special **Kid Vendors Market Day**, where youth entrepreneurs will be selling their wares!
