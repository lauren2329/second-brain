# VMP Department Structure

## Summary

Verdant Management Partners was organized into eight scalable departments designed to support clients such as Common Good Network, Kentucky NORML, and future nonprofits or small businesses.

## Departments

1. **Executive & Strategy** — vision, partnerships, leadership, legal oversight, growth, risk, and KPIs.
2. **Finance & Accounting** — bookkeeping, AP/AR, payroll, budgets, forecasts, reports, taxes, and cash management.
3. **Marketing & Communications** — brand, design, websites, social media, email, PR, content, and analytics.
4. **Operations & Administration** — projects, SOPs, admin support, vendors, purchasing, records, office management, and process improvement.
5. **Human Resources & Talent** — hiring, onboarding, training, performance, volunteers, employee relations, policies, and records.
6. **Compliance & Risk Management** — contracts, licensing, insurance, filings, policies, audits, data privacy, and organizational risk.
7. **Technology & Automation** — IT, Google Workspace, Monday, QuickBooks, AI workflows, hosting, backups, and software implementation.
8. **Client Success & Consulting** — onboarding, account management, advisory work, communication, meetings, implementation, reporting, and retention.

## Intended use

Each department can eventually own its own SOPs, KPIs, templates, and department head. The structure is broad enough to grow with VMP toward a larger management and consulting company.
