# Community Engagement Post Rewrite

## Summary

A recruitment post was rewritten to invite Bowling Green-area women to help launch Common Good Network. The message emphasized meaningful local impact, education, wellness, volunteerism, community relationships, and the opportunity to build something from the ground up.

## Messaging direction

- Write warmly and in the first person.
- Lead with meaningful community impact rather than organizational mechanics.
- Mention possible strengths such as event planning, outreach, administration, fundraising, and dependable hands-on help.
- Invite interested people to email **lauren@thecommongoodnetwork.org** and share what they care about.

## CGN description developed

CGN was described as a Kentucky-based 501(c)(3) that strengthens communities through education, wellness, volunteerism, charitable partnerships, and community engagement.

Several short social-profile versions under 255 characters were created. The favored style was:

> Building stronger communities through education, wellness, service, and collaboration. Common Good Network connects people, nonprofits, and businesses to create meaningful, lasting impact.
