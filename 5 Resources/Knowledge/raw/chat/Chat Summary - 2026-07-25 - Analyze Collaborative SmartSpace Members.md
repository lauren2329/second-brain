# Analyze Collaborative SmartSpace Members

## Summary

A May 18, 2026 WKU Innovation Campus directory was analyzed. It contained **76 total listings**:

- 37 Collaborative SmartSpace
- 20 Small Business Accelerator
- 18 WKU Innovation Campus Corridor
- 1 South Campus

## Confirmed independent nonprofits in the SmartSpace

Eight organizations were classified as confirmed independent nonprofits:

1. AccelerateKY
2. BG Resource Connection Village Inc.
3. Canopy
4. Community Ventures
5. Habitat for Humanity of Bowling Green/Warren County
6. Jack Dappa Blues Heritage Preservation Foundation
7. Kentucky To The World
8. LifeWorks for Autism

Three WKU units were excluded from the independent-nonprofit count. The Regional Technology Council and Civic Imagination Project were also excluded because separate tax-exempt status was not established.

## Pricing research

No published SmartSpace price or automatic WKU alumni discount was found.

- Estimated individual price: **$100–$200 per month**
- Suggested budgeting figure: **$150 per month**
- Possible setup/keycard charge: **$25–$75**
- Possible multi-user organization range: **$200–$500 per month**
- A negotiated alumni, startup, or nonprofit discount of **10–20%** was considered possible but unverified.
