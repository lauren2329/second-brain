# Grant Loan Fund Usage

## Summary

Application language was drafted for a bGenerous-style reimbursable grant loan. The funding was framed as short-term bridge financing for Common Good Network's launch of The Golden Door while it waits for contractual incentive funds.

## Proposed uses of funds

- Facility preparation and minor build-out
- Furniture, fixtures, and equipment
- Technology, security, and point-of-sale systems
- Gaming-related startup supplies
- Initial concessions and merchandise inventory
- Gaming payout reserves
- Licensing, compliance, insurance, and professional fees
- Initial marketing and community outreach
- Startup working capital

## Repayment-source explanation

The executed Lancaster Bingo Company Master ETABS Agreement was identified as supporting documentation. The order form provides for:

- A **$50,000 payment incentive** within 45 days of the effective date
- A **$40,000 credit** within 45 days
- Additional **$10,000 credits** in Years 2 and 3

The draft positioned the $50,000 incentive as the primary repayment source and ongoing charitable-gaming operations as additional support. It avoided treating vendor credits as cash available for loan repayment.

## Cautions

- Describe the funding as bridge financing, not unrestricted general operating money.
- Tie claims directly to the executed agreement.
- Do not overstate conditional reimbursements or restricted credits.
- Upload the executed ETABS agreement with the application.
