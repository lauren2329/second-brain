# Golden Door Revenue System

## Purpose

Build a diversified revenue portfolio around Golden Door while keeping gaming receipts, charitable support, retail sales, program revenue, restricted funds, and VMP service payments properly separated.

## Operating Assumptions

- Common Good Network Inc. (CGN) owns and operates Golden Door and its charitable gaming activity.
- Current operating coverage is expected from Lauren, Matthew, Colin, and possibly Mandy.
- Verdant Management Partners (VMP) is the only entity paid for services.
- VMP compensation requires a written scope, reasonable pricing, invoicing, conflict handling, and CGN governance approval.
- Supplemental revenue is not included in the base financial forecast until CGN approves amounts, timing, direct costs, and accounting treatment.

## Revenue Portfolio

| Category | Revenue Stream | Planning Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Charitable support | One-time donations | CGN charitable contribution revenue |
| Charitable support | Recurring donor program | CGN charitable contribution revenue |
| Charitable support | Grants | Track restricted and unrestricted awards separately |
| Charitable support | Qualified sponsorships | Acknowledgment benefits only unless advertising treatment is reviewed |
| Charitable support | Workplace giving, matching gifts, DAF and planned gifts | Longer-term donor-development channel |
| Venue and hospitality | Concessions and refreshments | Track sales tax, permits, inventory, and direct costs |
| Venue and hospitality | Reservations and private room rentals | Charge for space and amenities, not gaming access or outcomes |
| Venue and hospitality | Birthday and celebration packages | Space, decorations, concessions, and merchandise; exclude gaming consideration |
| Venue and hospitality | Special non-gaming events | Admission, facility-use, sponsorship, or concession revenue |
| Venue and hospitality | Vendor, pop-up, and food-truck fees or commissions | Written vendor and revenue-sharing terms |
| Retail | Branded merchandise | Track sales tax, inventory, and cost of goods |
| Retail | Co-branded partner merchandise | Document ownership and revenue-sharing terms |
| Retail | Vending and prepaid concession cards | Non-gaming purchases only |
| Mission programming | Workshops, classes, and program fees | Mission-related program-service revenue |
| Mission programming | Community resource events | Admission, grants, or sponsorships where appropriate |
| Regulated gaming expansion | Licensed bingo | Separate charitable-gaming scenario |
| Regulated gaming expansion | Licensed raffles | Separate charitable-gaming scenario |
| Regulated gaming expansion | Offsite special bingo or fundraising events | Separate licensing and event review |
| Regulated gaming expansion | Approved tournaments or special gaming events | Classify entry fees, prizes, and chance/skill elements before launch |
| Margin support | In-kind goods and services | Expense reduction; never count as cash revenue |

## Volunteer-Partner Support

CGN may make small, board-approved, mission-aligned donations or grants to partner organizations whose volunteers support on-site activities.

Those grants or donations must:

- Further CGN's charitable purpose.
- Follow written board-approved criteria.
- Be documented separately from volunteer schedules and VMP invoices.
- Not be calculated as an hourly wage or per-shift payment.
- Not be tied to gaming volume, prizes, results, or revenue.
- Not be described as compensation for charitable-gaming service.

## Accounting Categories

Maintain distinct classes or accounts for:

1. Charitable-gaming receipts.
2. Donations and recurring gifts.
3. Restricted and unrestricted grants.
4. Sponsorships.
5. Concessions.
6. Merchandise and inventory.
7. Facility rentals and reservations.
8. Program and event fees.
9. Sales-tax liabilities.
10. Direct costs by revenue stream.
11. VMP service payments.
12. Partner grants and donations.

## Modeling Rules

- The live workbook's `Revenue Streams` tab is the planning source of truth.
- Contract Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 gross-revenue inputs default to zero.
- Direct-cost percentages must be entered before relying on net contribution.
- Supplemental income does not change gaming AGR, Arrow fees, or gaming-retention calculations unless it is itself regulated gaming.
- New gaming formats require compliance review before revenue is forecast.
- In-kind support reduces expenses but is not cash revenue.
