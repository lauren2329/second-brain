## Decisions Behind the Overhead Model

### Rent
The executed lease with Elite Properties LLC is a 16-article Modified Triple Net lease dated July 9, 2026. The actual rent schedule is:

- Year 1: $3,500 per month
- Year 2: $3,750 per month
- Year 3: $4,000 per month

Any older model using a flat $4,000 monthly rent should be treated as superseded.

### VMP Service Fee
CGN does not budget separate direct chairperson payroll for Golden Door. Management and operating support are provided through VMP under a written service agreement.

Working escalation schedule:

- **Year 1: $3,500 per month** — held flat to protect cash during the survival-critical opening period.
- **Year 2: $5,000 per month** — contingent on operations clearing breakeven and maintaining required reserves.
- **Year 3: $6,500 per month** — contingent on sustained performance and still below the stated $9,500 ceiling.

The machine-count breakpoints in the VMP investor deal sheet have not yet been verified. The Year 1–3 schedule is therefore a planning assumption, not a finalized contractual tier determination.

The Year 2 and Year 3 increases should be performance-triggered rather than automatic calendar increases. If Golden Door is behind plan, the increase should be delayed.

### Marketing
The cash marketing budget is $250 per month for printing, networking, local outreach, and small direct expenses.

CGN also has access to approximately $10,000 per month in Google Ad Grant credits. Those credits belong to CGN generally, are not cash, and are not automatically allocated to Golden Door. Any allocation must be separately approved and tracked.

### No Bingo
Golden Door is planned as an electronic-pulltab-only operation. The location does not include floor space for bingo sessions, and excluding bingo avoids the extra callers, floor workers, scheduling, and operating complexity associated with traditional sessions.

## Costs Excluded From Gaming Revenue

### Video Surveillance
A video-surveillance system is required for electronic-pulltab monitoring across all three rooms under the KHRG compliance memo effective July 15, 2026.

Under 820 KAR 1:057 Section 14(4), this system is treated as not payable from charitable-gaming receipts. It must therefore be funded from a non-gaming source, such as:

- CGN unrestricted general funds
- A documented Verdant Ventures/VMP advance or loan
- A grant or donation that permits the expense
- Another lawful non-gaming source

The cost has not yet been sourced and is not included in fixed overhead.

### Statutory Retention Context

The runway model estimates that satisfying a 40% retention requirement under KRS 238.536 would require approximately **$897,810 in monthly gross receipts**, or about **5.16 times** the bare cash-survival figure.

This is a separate test from ordinary cash breakeven and must not be treated as interchangeable with the operating-overhead calculation.

## Cash Waterfall

1. Record gross gaming receipts in CGN.
2. Reserve prizes, distributor charges, state fees, and other mandatory gaming obligations.
3. Restore and protect the dedicated payout reserve.
4. Pay approved fixed operating overhead.
5. Restore the board-approved operating reserve.
6. Pay documented debt service from the responsible entity.
7. Pay the approved VMP service fee under contract.
8. Retain remaining funds for charitable purposes, capital needs, and board-approved expansion.

## Reserve Policy Draft

Opening and operating decisions should preserve enough unrestricted cash for:

- The dedicated $10,000 gaming-payout reserve.
- At least three months of fixed occupancy and essential overhead.
- One full month of VMP and other essential service obligations.
- Insurance deductibles and emergency repairs.
- Regulatory or licensing delays.
- At least one delayed distributor or incentive payment cycle.

Based on Year 1 fixed overhead, three months equals **$29,250**, before the separate payout reserve, variable gaming costs, debt service, buildout, signage, surveillance, or contingency spending.

## Financial Controls

- Maintain separate bank accounts and books for CGN and VMP.
- Do not pay personal expenses from CGN accounts.
- Do not treat restricted Arrow credit or Google ad credits as cash.
- Produce a weekly cash report during launch and stabilization.
- Produce a monthly board-level financial package.
- Require two-person approval for large or related-party payments.
- Document all loans, advances, reimbursements, management fees, and service arrangements.
- Reconcile gaming cash, payouts, machine reports, and deposits daily.
- Tie any VMP fee increase to documented financial performance and reserve compliance.
