# Calendar Rhythm

The calendar is the operating surface. The assistant prepares the blocks; Lauren shows up and works with the assistant or uses the block as accountability.

## Location hours

- Monday — Closed
- Tuesday–Thursday — 11:00 AM–7:00 PM
- Friday–Saturday — 11:00 AM–8:00 PM
- Sunday — 1:00 PM–5:00 PM

## Staffing assumptions

- Lauren — primary opener
- Matthew — primary closer
- Saturday opens — rotate Lauren/Matthew
- Sundays — rotate Lauren/Matthew
- Colin — add only after college schedule is known
- Mandy — not authorized; do not schedule

### Sunday — Close the Week
- 1:00–5:00 Location Open

## Calendar rules

- Put real work on the calendar, not vague intentions.
- Each block has one lane.
- The assistant prepares the block before it begins.
- Lauren is shown no more than three items at once.
- A missed block is rescheduled or reduced; it is not allowed to become an invisible failure.
- Monthly and quarterly rollups are folded into Monday CEO Day, not added as separate rituals.
