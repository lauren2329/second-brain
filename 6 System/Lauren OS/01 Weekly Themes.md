# Weekly Themes

These are lanes, not task lists. The assistant uses the day theme to decide what to surface.

## Monday — Money
- Personal money
- Family budget and bills
- VMP budget, invoices, cards, revenue
- CGN budget, funding, payouts, contracts
- Kentucky NORML budget and commitments

## Tuesday — Clients + Projects
- Active clients
- Deliverables
- Contracts
- Internal projects
- Deadlines
- Next milestones
- Waiting on

## Wednesday — Personal + Family
- Appointments
- Diet and groceries
- Grooming and wardrobe
- Workout and fitness tracking
- School, transportation, and household administration

## Thursday — Marketing + Sales
- Current marketing plans
- Sales goals
- Sponsor pipeline
- Campaigns
- Partnerships
- Events and long-lead outreach

## Friday — Operations + Reset
- Upcoming dates
- Compliance and renewals
- Systems and files
- Open loops
- Notebook sync
- Next week orientation

## Weekend
No standing work theme. Events, family, rest, or overflow only when deliberately chosen.

# Prepare the next theme

At the end of each day, the assistant opens tomorrow's lane and asks Lauren for an unstructured brain dump. It captures what comes to mind, identifies anything time-sensitive, and prepares the relevant digital dashboard for the next day.

- Sunday prepares Monday Money.
- Monday prepares Tuesday Clients + Projects.
- Tuesday prepares Wednesday Personal + Family.
- Wednesday prepares Thursday Marketing + Sales.
- Thursday prepares Friday Operations + Reset.
- Friday prepares the next week.
